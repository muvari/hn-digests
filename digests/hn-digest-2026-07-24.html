15 Databases

Postgres LISTEN/NOTIFY actually scales

300 points · 57 comments · dbos.dev

DBOS engineers push back on the popular claim that Postgres LISTEN/NOTIFY doesn't scale, tracing the real bottleneck to a global lock NOTIFY takes during transaction commit to preserve strict notification ordering — which capped their naive trigger-based implementation at ~2.9K stream writes/second regardless of available CPU or IOPS. By recognizing that notifications don't need to be a source of truth, they buffered and batched them in memory before periodically flushing in a single transaction, reaching 60K writes/second on one server.

What the comments said

  • Clarified repeatedly that this is an application-side buffering technique, not a patch to Postgres itself, and distinct from an unrelated notification-channel optimization landing in Postgres 19.
  • One commenter flagged the 8,000-byte NOTIFY payload limit as a real constraint this approach doesn't remove.
  • A broader philosophical thread debated that "scalability" is relative — 60K/s can be either wildly more or far less than a given system actually needs.
