Cloudflare acquires Deno

1,274 points · 660 comments · deno.com

Deno creator Ryan Dahl announced the entire Deno team is joining Cloudflare, folding their work into "celld," a distributed-application runtime built on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects. The Deno runtime gets only one more year of bugfix-only releases before development ends; Deno Deploy shuts down in six months with migration help for paying customers; and JSR keeps running, moved onto Cloudflare's infrastructure.

What the comments said

  • Many longtime Deno users were upset that the "we will end development of the Deno runtime after a year" detail was buried near the bottom of the post rather than headlined.
  • Debate over whether this is a loss for Deno or a win for Cloudflare's celld/workerd effort, with some reading it as effectively killing Deno to redirect the team's effort.
  • Some predicted the open-source runtime could be picked up by a foundation or forked by the community rather than simply dying.
  • Companies that recently migrated onto Deno felt burned, which several framed as the recurring risk of betting on a newer runtime over Node.
  • A more optimistic thread saw real promise in combining Deno's team with Cloudflare's Durable Objects for building AI agent infrastructure.
